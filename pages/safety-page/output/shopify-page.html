Cannabis Safety Information

At Golden Gardens, your safety is our priority. This page provides important guidance for responsible cannabis use.

Important: Cannabis products are intended for adults 21 and over. Keep all products out of reach of children and pets.

Before You Use

  • Consult with a healthcare professional if you take prescription medications or have underlying health conditions.
  • Do not use cannabis if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Start low and go slow – especially if you are new to cannabis or trying a new product type.
  • Read product labels carefully, including potency, serving size, and ingredients.

Responsible Consumption

  • Never drive or operate heavy machinery after consuming cannabis.
  • Do not mix cannabis with alcohol or other substances.
  • Wait at least two hours after consuming edibles before deciding to take more – effects can be delayed.
  • Store all cannabis products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Safe Storage

  • Keep all cannabis products in their original child-resistant packaging whenever possible.
  • Store in a locked container out of reach of children and pets.
  • Never leave cannabis products unattended in a vehicle.

Know Your Limits

Everyone reacts to cannabis differently. Factors such as body weight, tolerance, metabolism, and the method of consumption all affect how you experience cannabis. If you ever feel uncomfortable after consuming cannabis, move to a safe, calm environment, stay hydrated, and wait for the effects to subside.
